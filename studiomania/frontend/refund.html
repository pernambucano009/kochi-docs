Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

First subscription: 7-day refund

If this is your first subscription to any plan, you can request a full refund within 7 days of payment, provided you have used no more than 10% of the plan’s credits. Your account then returns to the free trial.

Monthly renewals

Renewals after that are non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time from the “My plan” page before the renewal date to avoid being charged again. After cancelling, your plan stays active until the end of the paid month.

Top-up credits

Top-ups are non-refundable once purchased and are valid for 12 months.

Errors on our side

  • Any generation that fails on our side has its credits returned to your balance automatically.
  • If you were charged by mistake or twice, we refund the full amount.
  • If the service was unavailable to you for an extended period because of us, contact us and we will compensate you.

How to request a refund

Email islam@simsimkit.com from the address registered on your account, with the payment date. We reply within 2 business days, and the money is returned to the original payment method within 5–14 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.
